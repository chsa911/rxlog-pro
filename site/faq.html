    Questions you might have

    ?: How long have you been doing it already? -> For almost 3 years.

    ?: Don't you miss the books that are gone? -> For me reading is a process that happens now and

    today. If I miss a book I get it on Amazon again. With that many books in store and prospective on the market there is no time to miss books.

    ?: Do you remember the books that you have read? -> The great ones yes. The fact that a book is a

    pageturner I (almost) never forget.

    ?: Why don't you use ebooks? -> Three main reasons:

    - Bigger choice: Not all paperbooks have been transfered to digital especially the older ones.

    - I love the haptic of paperbooks.

    - I can put my pages in any pocket and also sit on them.
